Following the meeting, a decision is made regarding whether an opportunity exists (00:06:20). If necessary, meetings with an adjusted focus can be conducted, or the lead can be transitioned to a contact in Insightly to end the process (00:06:46).
- **Opportunity Conversion and Team Assembly:** When an opportunity is confirmed, assistance can be requested to convert the lead into an opportunity within Insightly (00:07:19). - **Discussion Document Review and Client Alignment:** A decision is made regarding whether leadership review of the discussion document is required before meeting with the client (00:09:42).
